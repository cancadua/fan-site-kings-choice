Event Overview

Struggle for Supremacy (SfS) is a 4-week Alliance Season. Your alliance competes through different events and completes Alliance Goals, while individual players also earn progress toward Individual Milestones, Stage Rankings, and the overall Peak Ranking.

The key difference from normal ranking events is that your alliance needs to work together. You don't necessarily want everyone spending everything to chase their personal ranking — the goal is to maximize the alliance's overall result.

Current versions also let leaders see each member's contribution toward Alliance Goals, making coordination easier.

Participation

The current documented requirement is the Top 110 active Lords on Server Power Rankings for participation, while some rewards and progress remain available to ineligible players.

SfS participation rules have changed over time, so make sure you're using the rules shown in your current game version, rather than an old guide.

  1. ①

    Save before SfS starts

    Don't burn all your event resources immediately. Enter the season with a stockpile so you can push the events that matter most.

  2. ②

    Set one clear alliance goal

    Decide whether you're aiming for:

    • #1 overall
    • Top 3
    • A specific Stage Ranking
    • Alliance Goals/rewards
    • An MVP/title

    Don't waste resources trying to achieve everything.

  3. ③

    Coordinate spending

    Before each relevant event, tell members whether to push or save. A coordinated 20-player push is much stronger than everyone randomly spending resources.

  4. ④

    Don't let everyone compete individually

    Choose a few strong players to seriously chase the individual/Peak ranking. Everyone else should prioritize Alliance Goals and efficient contribution.

  5. ⑤

    Watch Alliance Goal progress

    Use the member-progress information to see who is contributing and what the alliance still needs.

  6. ⑥

    Save your biggest resources for the right moment

    Don't spend a huge stockpile just because you can rank #1 in one small event. Look at the overall 4-week payoff.

  7. ⑦

    Communicate every day

    Alliance leadership should post something simple like:

    • PUSH: spend resources in this event
    • SAVE: don't spend, wait for the next event
    • TARGET: we need X more Alliance Goal progress
  8. ⑧

    Don't chase an unwinnable ranking

    If a much stronger cross-server alliance is massively ahead, don't throw months of saved resources away just to finish slightly higher. Secure the rewards you can realistically obtain.

  9. ⑨

    Think 4 weeks, not 1 day

    The best SfS alliance isn't necessarily the one that spends the most — it is the one that spends its resources at the right times.

    Best rule: coordinate first → save resources → push together → stop when the next reward isn't worth the cost.
