Event Overview

RWYS is an Alliance event followed by a Cross-Server stage using the same mechanics.

The goal is to:

  • Explore to obtain crops and items.
  • Plant crops to earn points.
  • Expand the alliance island.
  • Build statues that upgrade your crops.
  • Defend your crops.
  • Loot enemy alliances' crops.

Resources

Exploring costs 1 Stamina and always gives:

  • 100 Exploration EXP boosted by your expedition knights boost (0-20%)
  • 1 Tile Material or Statue Material
  • 1 random Crop or Item

Stamina:

  • Max 28
  • +4 every 30 minutes

Exploration Level

Higher Exploration Level increases the chance of getting higher rarity crops.

Increase it by:

  • Exploring
  • Delegating up to 4 knights (uses Total Talents only)

Maximum EXP bonus from delegation: +20%

Crop Rarity

Flawed → Common → Fine → Excellent → Rare → Exquisite → Perfect → Legendary

Higher rarity means:

  • More points
  • Longer production
  • Better loot

Items

Blessing Tree

Multiple trees stack to provide cumulative benefits.

Hourglass

Speeds up nearby crops

Loot Tool

Used to attack enemy crops

Healing Potion

Revives injured knights

Combat

Only Total Talents determine knight strength.

Tile defenses:

  • Up to 5 knights
  • Traps activate before battle

  1. ①

    Explore, explore, explore!

    • Stay active, keep your moves away from overflow.

    More moves, more score. More moves, higher level, even more score.

  2. ②

    Delegate knights

    • Delegate your knights to have 20% more bonus EXP for exploring.
    • Knight Total Talents are the only combat stat that matters.

    Always target to have up to 20% bonus, no more, find smaller knight alternatives if possible.

  3. ③

    Plant carefully

    Before placing a crop, try to activate:

    • Preferred terrain (-10% production time)
    • Neighbor Bonus #1 (+10%)
    • Neighbor Bonus #2 (+10%)

    Don't rush Purple/Yellow/Orange crops — high rarity crops are worth waiting for.

    Save resources for expensive crops:

    • Save Blessing Trees for Yellow+ crops.
    • Save Hourglasses for expensive crops.
    • Save Loot Tools for the last day.

    Stacked bonuses can produce massive amount of score.

  4. ④

    Defend valuable crops

    Protect Purple+ crops with:

    • Strong talent knights
    • Traps

    Don't waste defenses on low-value crops — ignore them if necessary.

  5. ⑤

    Expand Land & Build Statues

    Spend materials throughout the event, keep common focus with the alliance.

    Expand and contribute to statues and land unlock tiles whenever materials are available — both improve your alliance's farming options and provide rewards.

  6. ⑥

    Loot wisely

    Loot the highest-value crops available.

    Later in the event, enemies have far more high-rarity crops, making Loot Tools much more valuable.

}
