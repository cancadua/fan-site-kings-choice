Advanced Tips & Strategies

Concentrate Knight Power

Some game mechanics require extremely high attributes concentrated into a single knight. Identify which of your knights should be your "powerhouse" and focus resources there. A single maxed knight often outweighs a balanced army.

Balance Attribute Types

Different game elements favor different attributes. Some require Strength dominance, others need Intellect or Charisma. Build a roster of specialized knights rather than generalizing. This flexibility is key to winning across all events.

Optimize Resource Spending

Calculate the cost-effectiveness of each upgrade path. Spending silver on a low-tier knight might be wasteful; focus investments on knights that will see long-term use and high-value roles.

Time Your Events

Coordinate your strongest efforts with high-reward event windows. Some events yield much better rewards than others—plan your upgrades to peak during these critical events.

Track Equipment Paths

Plan your equipment merging sequence far in advance. Accidentally using the wrong material can waste weeks of progress. Keep spreadsheets of your equipment pipeline to avoid costly mistakes.

Join an Active Alliance

Alliance members amplify your progression through shared benefits and organized event participation. A coordinated alliance can collectively dominate events and secure better territories.
