Privacy

Visitor statistics

To see how many people use the site and which pages are useful, I use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address and does not follow you across other websites.

It records only anonymous, aggregated information such as:

  • which page was opened and where the visit came from (referrer),
  • browser, operating system and device type, screen size, language,
  • your country (worked out from the IP address, which is not stored),
  • a few actions: choosing an event, creating a shared map and clicking the Ko-fi button.

Query strings, including shared map codes, are left out. If your browser sends the "Do Not Track" signal, nothing is recorded.

Accounts (Alliance tools)

If you create an account, the site stores your e-mail address, username and a hashed password, plus the alliance and player data you or your alliance enter. This is used only to run the Alliance tools. It is stored in a database on a third-party host and is not sold or shared for advertising.

The Alliance tools are a private notebook for alliance leaders. Everything in them is entered by hand. They are not connected to King's Choice, read no game data, never ask for your game account or password, and cannot see or change anything in the game.

Shared maps

A shared Uncharted Waters map stores only the city list and an expiry time. The share code is stored only as a hash, and the map is deleted automatically when it expires.

Local storage

Your browser may keep small preferences on your device, such as the last event you viewed or whether you dismissed the announcement banner. They never leave your device.

Ko-fi

The Ko-fi button opens Ko-fi in a new tab. Payments and any data you give there are handled by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask what is stored about your account or to have it deleted, contact me through my Ko-fi page.
