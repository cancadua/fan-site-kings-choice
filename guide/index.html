Advanced Progression Guide

Prioritize Leadership Experience (EXP)

Your Lord Level is the foundation of all progression in Kings Choice. At every decision point, prioritize Leadership EXP over basic resources. Increasing your Lord Level unlocks resource collection caps, grants access to more powerful knights, and opens advanced gameplay features. A higher Lord Level compounds your advantage exponentially.

Master Prestige for Competitive Edge

Prestige is your competitive advantage in a multiplayer world. Higher Prestige translates to:

  • Winning more duels in Negotiation (essential for resource trading)
  • Starting Uncharted Waters events with bonus Voyager Coins
  • Claiming and defending Dragon Island camps against rival Lords
  • Defending your realm from invasion and conquest

Farm Prestige aggressively through event participation, especially during high-reward events like Uncharted Waters and Dragon Island.

Event Participation is Core Strategy

Events are where the game's true progression happens. Take an active role in every event to obtain the majority of upgrade materials needed for your knights and earn serious Prestige gains. Casual players progress slowly; active event participants advance exponentially. Plan your schedule to participate fully in events.

Knight Equipment Management

Once knights reach level 100, Knight Equipment becomes essential to raise their level cap further. Equipment can be merged to create higher-rank pieces, but plan carefully to avoid depleting lower-grade equipment. Always keep an eye on your knight's upcoming equipment needs and manage your inventory strategically.
